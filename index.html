Pathway

You are given two points -- A and B. Between them is a forest. Your task is to find the quickest and safest path through it.

The screen is split into two parts. The top is the choosing area, where the forest and both points are visible. The bottom is the panel, where your controls live.

To build your path, tap anywhere in the choosing area. Each tap places a waypoint, numbered in order. The path connects each point with a straight line. To remove your last waypoint, use Undo. To start over, use Clear.

When you are ready, tap Submit. The system will assess the safety and time taken to travel from A to B.

Author Note

Pathway is a game about finding solace. Essentially, solace isn't a destination you reach through a straight line. It takes turns and repetition to figure out what works for you.

I have been looking for paths to get out of feeling stuck. There is a gap between knowing and making my path out of the forest. And intention alone doesn't get me out of it, only recently have I come to terms with getting help and hoping for a path out.

So I hope Pathway brings about some solace to you as well. If anything, I hope it's a fun puzzle for you to master.
